How to Not Get Repo'd

Body

WASD walk · Shift sprint · Space jump · C crouch

Hold LMB GRAB: loot, or any steep rock to climb it (watch your stamina). Space while climbing = lunge.

RMB throw, or shove empty-handed · Q drop · wheel distance

E use: seats, door, winch, bell, buttons, keypad, bunk · hold on a knocked-out friend to pick them up

MMB ping · 1-6 emotes

Talking

Use the game's voice chat, not a call: voices carry about 25 m, from where you stand, and walls muffle them. Past that, only walkie-talkies get through (T, any range, to anyone else with one: $150 each at the store).

The RV

Each morning picks a driver and a navigator (the party frames say who). Only the driver takes the wheel (anyone, while they're away): E on the driver's seat, WASD drive, Space brake, H horn.

Winch: E the front bumper to take the hook, carry it to a post or dead tree, E to clip it, then the driver hits R to reel in.

Walk into the RV to push it; crouch inside to brace for a crash. The driver can't see the road map on the dash: the navigator takes it (F raises it) and calls out the road.

Crashes dent the RV. Broken down, hold E at its back; Gus fixes it in town.

The money

One shared bank. Pawn loot in town, gamble at the casino, and pay the Repo Man by midnight, every night. Miss one payment and he takes the RV: the trip is over.
